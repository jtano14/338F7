Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

FLUX7 Co., Ltd. ("FLUX7," "we," "our") respects the privacy of people who contact us through our website and business activities. This policy explains how we handle personal information.

1. Information we handle

We may receive your name, company, job title, email address, telephone number, inquiry details, and information you choose to share in business correspondence. Basic technical information may also be processed by our website hosting provider when you access the site.

2. Purposes of use

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, discuss proposed consulting or trading engagements, evaluate business opportunities, communicate with existing or prospective business partners, manage business relationships, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use inquiry information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

3. Contact form and email

Our current contact form prepares a message in your own email application. It does not itself upload the completed inquiry to a FLUX7 web server. When you send the message, it is transmitted through your chosen email provider to our business mailbox. Your email provider's practices may also apply.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with service providers that support business communications and operations, and with relevant business counterparties when necessary to address your inquiry, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. International business communications

Because FLUX7 works across international markets, responding to a business inquiry may involve communicating with parties outside Japan. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we will address applicable legal requirements before doing so.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purposes, business records, dispute handling, or legal requirements, and then delete or anonymize it as appropriate.

7. Your requests

For questions or requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of personal information, contact us at info@flux7.co.jp. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify identity.

8. Website technologies

The website may use technical services necessary to deliver pages and embedded resources. We will update this policy if additional analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are introduced that require further disclosure.
